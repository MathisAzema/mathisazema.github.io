I am a PhD candidate in applied mathematics at [CERMICS](https://cermics-lab.enpc.fr/), École des Ponts, under the supervision of [Vincent Leclère](https://leclere.github.io/) and Wim van Ackooij (EDF R&D).

My research focuses on decomposition methods for stochastic, robust and distributionally robust optimization, particularly for mixed-integer problems arising in power systems. I work on strengthening Benders and SDDP cuts, infinite-horizon SDDP methods, and Lagrangian reformulations for robust optimization with mixed-integer recourse.

Before that, I graduated from [École polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/) (X2020, ranked 10th out of 425 students) and earned master's degrees in Optimization and Data Science at ENSTA Paris and in Operations Research at the Conservatoire National des Arts et Métiers ([MPRO](https://uma.ensta-paris.fr/mpro/)).
